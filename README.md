## 🌌 About the Project

**AstroQuest** is an interactive educational web experience that turns space science into an exciting learning adventure.

Travel across the Moon, Mars, and deep space while discovering the stories behind NASA's spacecraft, scientific instruments, and other space hardware. Explore the purpose of these machines, learn about their missions and achievements, and uncover the history of equipment left behind after completing its mission.

Inspired by the challenge **"Abandoned but Not Forgotten,"** AstroQuest aims to bring these forgotten pieces of space exploration history back into the spotlight through engaging, scroll-based storytelling.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Storytelling** – Discover the stories and history behind NASA's space hardware.
- **Space Exploration** – Explore locations across the Moon, Mars, and deep space.
- **Educational Content** – Learn about spacecraft, instruments, their purposes, and their scientific achievements.
- **Scroll-Based Experience** – Navigate through the story using your mouse wheel or touchpad.

## 🚀 Getting Started

AstroQuest is a web-based application. **No installation or downloads are required.**

### Accessing the Application

1. Open the AstroQuest website through your preferred web browser.
2. Wait for the application to load.
3. Start your journey through space!

## 🎮 How to Use

AstroQuest primarily uses scroll-based navigation to guide users through the experience.

| Action | Function |
|---|---|
| Scroll Down | Progress through the story and explore the next sections. |
| Scroll Up | Return to previously explored sections. |
| Mouse Wheel / Touchpad | Navigate through the experience. |

**Recommended:** Use a desktop or laptop with a modern web browser for the best experience.
