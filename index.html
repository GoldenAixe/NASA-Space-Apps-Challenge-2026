    The Incomplete One

    Kite is an exploration robot sent to Mars… but a navigation failure hurled it onto the Moon, scattering its parts and damaging its memory. Repair Kite with discoveries inspired by real NASA missions, and get it back on course for the Red Planet.

    Scroll up to move forward and down to go back. When you are close to a glowing item, collect it. Kite will tell you the story of every item it collects, and each one restores part of Kite while revealing a piece of real space exploration history. On touch screens, drag up or down. Keep your sound on to hear Kite’s story.

    🔈 Tap or press any key to hear Kite tell the story.

    Mission Complete!

    Kite is whole again, carrying a gift from every machine it met. Choose Kite’s next chapter — or keep exploring at your own pace.
