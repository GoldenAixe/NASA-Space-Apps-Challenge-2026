    The Incomplete One

    Kite is an exploration robot sent to Mars to help scientists prepare for future human missions. But a spacecraft malfunction sends Kite crashing onto the Moon, scattering its parts and damaging its memory. Help Kite repair itself, learn from the explorers who came before, and finally reach the Red Planet.

    Scroll up to move forward and down to go back. Click each glowing discovery when you are close. Every discovery restores part of Kite and reveals a piece of real space exploration history. On touch screens, drag up or down. Turn on sound to hear Kite tell its story.
